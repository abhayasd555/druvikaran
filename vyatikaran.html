      Model. Slits use exact paraxial Fresnel diffraction of a Gaussian laser beam (0.5 mm radius) through 3 mm tall slits, so the pattern changes properly from near field to far field as you move the screen. Gratings send copies of the beam into each order at the true angle, with the efficiency of an ideal amplitude grating. Interferometers add two Gaussian beams with their real offsets, tilts, curvatures, path lengths and polarisations; fringe visibility falls with path difference as exp(−(ΔP/Lc)²). A mirror tilt of α turns a beam by 2α. The white LED is summed over 15 wavelengths.

      Single photons are heralded pairs from down-conversion, fibre-coupled into the same Gaussian mode as the laser, so they follow the same pattern: each detection is drawn at random from it. Coherence length is λ²/Δλ. Detectors have 60% efficiency; the camera has a few dark counts.
