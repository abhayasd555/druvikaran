Align the polarisation

Each round the fibre scrambles the input polarisation and a new target appears. Rotate the λ/4, λ/2 and λ/4 paddles to steer the red point into the violet ring before time runs out.

  • Hold a rotate button (or key) to turn a paddle; tap for a small step.
  • Keys: Q/A paddle 1, W/S paddle 2, E/D paddle 3. Hold Shift for fine control.
  • Each level shortens the clock and shrinks the target ring.

Paddle controller

Paddle angle sets the waveplate's fast axis around the fibre.

Model: each paddle is a fixed-retardance waveplate (λ/4, λ/2, λ/4) whose fast axis sits at angle θ around the fibre. On the Poincaré sphere it rotates the Stokes vector by its retardance about the axis (cos 2θ, sin 2θ, 0) in the equatorial plane. The S₃ > 0 hemisphere is labelled right-circular.
