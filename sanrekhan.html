Mirror mounts

Each kinematic mount has two thumbscrews. One full turn tilts the mirror by 8 mrad.

Model: a 1 mm diameter beam (1/e²) from a 5 mW laser. Mirror 1 sits 150 mm before mirror 2, iris 1 is 250 mm after mirror 2, and iris 2 is 600 mm further on. A mirror tilt of α deflects the beam by 2α. Transmission through each iris is the fraction of the Gaussian beam inside the aperture. Diffraction is ignored.
