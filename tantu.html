Steering and focus

Three mounts

The mirrors have fine-thread screws: one turn tilts the mirror 2 mrad. The fibre's focus screw moves it 250 µm per turn.

Model: a 633 nm laser of 2 mW is steered by two mirrors 150 mm apart, the second 200 mm before an 11 mm focal-length asphere. The single-mode fibre's mode field radius is 2.2 µm. Coupling is the product of the mode-overlap terms for sideways offset at the fibre face, the beam's angle to the fibre axis (set by where it crosses the lens), and defocus, times 85% for lens aberrations and Fresnel losses. A mirror tilt of α deflects the beam by 2α.
