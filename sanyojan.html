Steering the green beam

Two mounts

A steering mirror, then the dichroic itself. One full turn tilts either by 8 mrad.

Model: the steering mirror sits 120 mm before the dichroic, and the cameras sample the combined beam 300 mm and 900 mm after it. A mount tilt of α deflects the reflected green beam by 2α. The red beam passes through the dichroic's 3 mm glass substrate (n = 1.5), so tilting the dichroic shifts red sideways by about 8 µm per turn, without changing its direction. Overlap is the Gaussian mode overlap of the two beams at each camera, multiplied together and divided by the best possible for these beam sizes.
